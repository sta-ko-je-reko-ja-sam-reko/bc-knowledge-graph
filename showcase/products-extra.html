Serbian Localization private repository

A complete Serbian localization for Business Central W1 (version 29), built as one per-tenant extension on the standard E-Document framework.

  • SEF e-invoicing: invoices, credit and debit notes, advance invoices, status updates, and receiving, approving and rejecting purchase invoices
  • VAT: VAT setup with POPDV codes, SEF VAT records, the POPDV statement and reconciliation with the tax administration's calculation
  • e-Otpremnica: outgoing, incoming and internal despatch advices
  • Finance: the official chart of accounts, Bilans stanja and Bilans uspeha with APR export, IOS and compensation
  • Banking: payments with model 97 references and Halcom payment orders, Halcom bank statement import, NBS exchange rates
  • Fixed assets: tax depreciation, including the legacy group method, and the POA report
  • Identifiers: PIB, matični broj and JBKJS checks
  • Serbian (Latin) translation and a setup hub with assisted setup per feature
